Questions

The fine print.

Is it really custom, or a template?+

Really custom. Every Ode is designed from scratch around the person it's for — their interests become the actual concept of the site, not just photos in a frame. That's the whole point, and it's why no two look alike.

When do I pay?+

Only after you've seen it. We build your Ode, send you a private preview, and you decide. Love it, it's $5. Don't, and it never goes live. You're not charged.

Can I change the message?+

Yes — up to three rounds of edits to the wording before you publish. Getting the words exactly right is usually the part that matters most, so we give you room to fuss over it.

How do I send it to them?+

Once you publish, you get a private link to your Ode. Send it however you like — text, email, or dropped into a card. They tap it and they're in; no app to download, no account to make.

How long does it take?+

Less than 24 hours from when you send us the details to when your private preview lands in your inbox. Need it sent today? Just let us know and we'll turn it around.
