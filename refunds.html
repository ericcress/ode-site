We build your Ode and send you a private preview before you pay anything. If it doesn't feel right, don't publish it — you're never charged. You can also ask us to refine your message and details before you decide.

If you publish your Ode and it doesn't turn out to be the gift you hoped for, email us within 14 days and we'll issue a full refund. The published Ode is then made inaccessible.

How to request a refund

Email hello@ode.gift from the address you ordered with, and tell us the recipient's name. We'll take care of it.

Timing

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
