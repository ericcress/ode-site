Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy explains what Aurea LLC ("Ode," "we," "us") collects when you use ode.gift, and how we use it. We keep it simple: we collect what we need to build your gift, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

  • Order details — your name and email, the recipient's name and occasion, the story and message you write, and any photos you upload.
  • Payment information — handled by Stripe. We receive a confirmation that a payment succeeded; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic technical data — standard server and delivery information (such as approximate request data) needed to operate and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To design, build, host, and deliver your Ode.
  • To email you about your order (your preview link, receipts, and support).
  • To process your payment and honor our guarantee.

Who we share it with

We use a small set of trusted service providers to run Ode, and share only what each needs:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Postmark — sending order and preview emails.
  • Cloudflare and Railway — hosting, delivery, and security.

We do not sell your personal information or your photos, and we don't use them for advertising.

Your photos

Photos you upload are used only to create and host your Ode. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your order details and hosted Ode for up to one year, or until you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your information by emailing hello@ode.gift. Ode is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date will change when we do. Questions? hello@ode.gift.
